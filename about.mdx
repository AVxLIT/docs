Virani International LLC is a privately held, diversified operating and property company with active businesses in the United States and India. We operate across retail, fuel, coin-operated amusement machines (COAM), and commercial real estate — sectors we have entered, built, and continued to grow through direct ownership and hands-on management.

## Who We Are

Virani International is built on entrepreneurship and ownership. We are operators first — we build businesses, run them ourselves, and invest in properties that generate long-term value. Our experience spans multiple countries and sectors, and our decisions are guided by a straightforward set of principles: invest where we understand the market, manage what we own, and take a long-term view on capital. We do not chase trends; we look for durable businesses and properties in markets we know well.

## Our Approach

We operate with a hands-on model. Across every business in our portfolio — whether a retail store in Georgia or a commercial property in Mumbai — ownership is active, not passive. This gives us direct insight into operations, costs, and customer relationships, and it means we are accountable for outcomes in a way that passive investors are not.

Diversification is a deliberate choice, not an accident of opportunity. Our portfolio spans retail, fuel distribution, COAM, and commercial real estate across two countries. Each of these sectors behaves differently across economic cycles, and that breadth gives our overall portfolio a degree of stability that a single-sector focus would not.

We deploy capital with a long time horizon. We are not building toward a near-term exit; we are building businesses and accumulating properties that compound in value over time. That orientation shapes how we make decisions — including when to be patient, when to expand, and when to hold.

## Our Footprint

**United States**

Our US operations are concentrated in the Southeast and South. In Georgia, we operate four retail locations and a fuel station with COAM. In North Carolina, we run two fuel stations with COAM operations. Texas is home to one retail location. Together, these represent a set of businesses that we have built and continue to manage directly.

**India**

Our Indian portfolio is centered in Mumbai, where we operate five retail properties — including a presence at JP Connect Mira Road and JP North The Palace. We also operate one retail location in Delhi. A number of our commercial properties in India are leased to established businesses, reflecting our approach of combining active retail operations with income-generating property holdings.

Our roots in India run deep: the business began there with motorcycle seat-cover manufacturing before expanding internationally, and India remains a core part of what we do.
