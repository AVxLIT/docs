## What to Include

To help us respond promptly and accurately, please structure your inquiry as follows before reaching out.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your Name and Company">
    Introduce yourself with your full name and the name of your organization or company, if applicable. This allows us to address you appropriately and understand the context of your inquiry.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your Contact Information">
    Include a reliable email address and, where appropriate, a phone number. We will use these details to follow up directly and ensure our response reaches the right person.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Inquiry Type">
    Indicate which category your inquiry falls under — General, Property & Leasing, Business Opportunity, Partnership, Vendor / Supplier, or Other. This helps us route your message without delay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="A Brief Description of Your Inquiry">
    Provide a clear and concise overview of your inquiry, proposal, or question. There is no need for exhaustive detail at this stage — a few sentences outlining what you are seeking and why is sufficient.
  </Step>
</Steps>

We aim to acknowledge all serious inquiries within a reasonable timeframe and will follow up with the appropriate next steps where there is a genuine fit. Virani International is committed to engaging thoughtfully with every inquiry we receive.
