Virani International has built a portfolio of commercial and retail properties across the United States and India — assets that serve a dual purpose: anchoring the company's own operating businesses and providing stable commercial space to established tenants. From retail corridors in Georgia and Texas to mixed-use locations across Mumbai, each property is held as a long-term asset, selected for its alignment with markets where Virani International maintains an active operational presence.

## Our Property Portfolio

Virani International's approach to property ownership is deliberate and market-driven. Rather than acquiring properties speculatively, the company owns real estate in the same geographies where it operates retail, fuel, and commercial businesses. This creates a natural alignment between the physical assets and the businesses they support. The portfolio is managed with a long-term hold philosophy — properties are maintained as productive, income-generating assets, whether occupied by Virani-operated businesses or leased to established commercial tenants.

## Property Management Philosophy

Virani International approaches property ownership as an extension of its operating business — each asset is selected because it serves a specific commercial purpose, whether housing a company-operated business or providing quality space to a vetted commercial tenant. The company favors long-term ownership over frequent turnover, allowing properties to mature as stable, productive assets within their local markets. Active oversight of each location ensures that properties are maintained to the standard expected of a business that depends on them for its own daily operations.
