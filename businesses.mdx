With an active presence across multiple US states — Georgia, Texas, and North Carolina — and a growing footprint in India's Mumbai and Delhi markets, the company brings a hands-on, owner-operated approach to each vertical it enters.

## About COAM Operations

Coin-operated amusement machines (COAM) are licensed electronic gaming and entertainment terminals commonly found at gas stations and convenience stores in states where they are legally regulated. In Georgia and North Carolina, COAM operations are subject to state licensing requirements and oversight, ensuring compliant, regulated play for adult patrons. Virani operates COAM terminals at select fuel and convenience locations as a regulated ancillary service alongside core fueling and retail offerings.
