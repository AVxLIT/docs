## Geographic Footprint

Virani International operates across two countries, with US operations concentrated in the Southeast and South, and Indian operations centered in Mumbai and Delhi.

| Country | Locations |
|---------|-----------|
| United States | Georgia, Texas, North Carolina |
| India | Mumbai, Delhi |

**United States**

Georgia anchors our US presence with four retail locations and a fuel station with COAM operations. We also operate a retail location in Texas and two fuel stations with COAM in North Carolina.

**India**

Our Indian portfolio includes five retail properties in Mumbai and one in Delhi. Our Mumbai operations include an office at JP Connect Mira Road and a store at JP North The Palace. Several commercial properties are leased to established businesses, including Jockey and pharmacy tenants.
